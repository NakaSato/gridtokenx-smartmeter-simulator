Simulation Lifecycle

1

Initialization

The system initializes N smart meters with unique configurations (Solar capacity, Battery size, Location).

2

Tick Loop (5s)

Every 5 seconds, the engine triggers a "tick". Meters calculate their current generation based on time of day and weather.

3

Energy Calculation

Generation = Capacity * SunFactor * Weather
Consumption = BaseLoad * TimeFactor

P2P Energy Trading

arrow_upward Seller (Prosumer)

Has surplus energy (Gen > Cons).

  • • Calculates Surplus = Gen - Cons
  • • Sets Sell Price (e.g., $0.15/kWh)
  • • Broadcasts offer to market

arrow_downward Buyer (Consumer)

Has energy deficit (Cons > Gen).

  • • Calculates Deficit = Cons - Gen
  • • Sets Buy Price (e.g., $0.20/kWh)
  • • Matches with lowest sell offers

Cryptographic Security

vpn_key Key Management

Each smart meter generates a unique ECDSA key pair upon initialization. The private key never leaves the meter.

verified Digital Signatures

Every energy reading is signed with the meter's private key. This ensures Non-repudiation and Data Integrity.

Why is this important?

In a decentralized grid, we must trust the data source. Cryptography prevents malicious actors from spoofing meter readings to steal energy credits.
